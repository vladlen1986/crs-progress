## Managing your plan

Bubble [plans](https://bubble.io/pricing) are applied to a specific app, rather than to your account. As such, you can flexibly change the plan of one app to facilitate for different stages of development and growth.

Upgrading your plan offers more features and increases the app's included workload. Here, you can switch between plans and view the features each one entails. Additionally, any active coupons associated with your account will be visible here.

All payments are processed through Stripe.

### Workload add-ons

Besides the workload that comes included in your plan, you also have the option to add more workload to continue scaling.

* **Workload overages:** To meet temporary demands, you can pay-as-you-go for what your app uses. Overages are automatically enabled to ensure your app stays live during period of high user demand, but you can disable it any time by unchecking the *Overages are enabled* box.
* **Workload subscription:** Workload subscriptions offer volume-based discounts. You can add a subscription by clicking *Add more workload*, which will show you the different tiers available.

#### Workload billing timing:

While your plan and workload tier subscriptions are billed on your regular billing date, workload overages are calculated and billed separately at the end of each calendar month.

This means you can subscribe to a workload subscription any time before the last day of the month to apply the volume discount.

### Paid plugins

This section of *Settings* will show any paid [plugins](https://bubble.io/plugins) that the current app is subscribed to.
