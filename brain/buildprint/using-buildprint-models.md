# Using Buildprint models
> Source: https://docs.buildprint.ai/using-buildprint-models-3zmc7 · Captured: 2026-07-14 (verbatim .md)

Buildprint models let you use Buildprint agents without connecting your own Claude, ChatGPT/Codex, or Gemini subscription.

![Image](https://static.ferndesk.com/user-images/file_01KSAQNP412K2EA6BDZ4D5GZMP.jpg)

## How it works

Buildprint models are pay-as-you-go AI models billed through Buildprint credits.

Workspaces include a monthly amount of Buildprint AI credit. The product currently shows $5 per month of included Buildprint AI credit for workspaces.

Using your own connected Claude, ChatGPT/Codex, or Gemini subscription does not use Buildprint AI credits.

If your workspace has an OpenRouter key connected, Buildprint models use that OpenRouter key instead of Buildprint AI credits.

## When to use Buildprint models

Buildprint models are useful if you:

- Do not want to connect a separate AI subscription.
- Want another model option for occasional agent work.
- Want workspace-level usage through Buildprint credits.
- Want to use a workspace OpenRouter key for Buildprint model traffic.

For heavier usage, connecting your own AI subscription or OpenRouter key may give you more direct control over limits and billing.

## Add credits

1. Go to Agent -> Integrations -> AI Providers.
2. Find the Buildprint provider card.
3. Click **Add credits**.

You can also view AI credit usage from Workspace settings -> Billing.

## Credit errors

If credits run out, Buildprint will ask you to connect ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or OpenRouter, or add credits before continuing with Buildprint models.
