# Casino Settings — User Manual

> **Who this is for:** owners and administrators using the Casino Settings page in CRS to manage their company details and casino properties.
> **What it covers:** what the page is for, how to edit company information, how to add and manage properties, and why some things may be locked for you.
> **Last updated:** 2026-06-08

---

## What this page is for

Casino Settings is where you manage the two top levels of your organization in CRS:

- **Your company** — the business that owns the casino(s): its name, registration and licence numbers, contact details, logo, and default currency.
- **Your properties** — each physical casino location: its name, logo, timezone, and reporting currency.

You set these once, and the rest of CRS uses them — for example, a property's timezone drives how times are shown, and its reporting currency is the one figures are recorded in.

You'll only ever see and manage **your own company and properties** — never another company's.

---

## The page at a glance

- **Company section** — your company's details: name, registration number, VAT number, licence number, email, phone, logo, and default currency.
- **Properties section** — the list of casinos belonging to your company. From here you can add a new property and open one to edit its details (name, logo, timezone, reporting currency) or change whether it's active.
- Each section has its own **Save** — changes aren't stored until you save.

---

## How to do common tasks

### Edit your company details
1. Open the **Company** section.
2. Update the fields you need (name, numbers, contact details, default currency).
3. Click **Save**.
*(You need the "Company – Edit" permission. If you don't have it, the fields are view-only.)*

### Change or remove the company logo
1. Upload a new image in the logo area, **or** click **Remove Logo** to clear it.
2. Save if prompted.
*(Logo changes also require "Company – Edit.")*

### Add a property
1. In the **Properties** section, click **Add Property**.
2. Fill in the property's name and details (timezone, reporting currency, logo).
3. Confirm — the new property is created under your company automatically.
*(You need the "Property – Create" permission.)*

### Edit a property
1. Open the property from the list.
2. Update its fields.
3. Click **Save**.
*(You need the "Property – Edit" permission.)*

### Activate or deactivate a company or property
1. Open the company or property.
2. Use the **Active / Inactive** toggle and save.
*(Changing active status is a separate permission — "Company – Deactivate" or "Property – Deactivate." You may be able to edit details but not change the active status, or vice-versa, depending on your role.)*

## Choosing currencies and timezones

- **Default Currency (on the company)** is your head-office fallback currency.
- **Reporting Currency (on each property)** is the currency that property's figures are actually recorded in — this is the operational source of truth for that casino.
- **Timezone (on each property)** sets the property's local time. Pick the city/region that matches the casino's location.

The currency and timezone pickers are searchable — start typing to narrow the long list.

## Who can do what (and why some things are locked)

Casino Settings respects the same three levels of access as the rest of CRS:

- **Owner** — full control over every company and property.
- **Administrator** — manages their own company and properties, according to the specific permissions their role has (edit, create, deactivate).
- **Everyone else** — if your role doesn't include permission to view this page, you'll see an **"Access Restricted"** screen instead.

Because edit, create, and deactivate are **separate permissions**, your role might let you do some of these but not others. For example, you might be able to edit a property's name but not deactivate it. If a control is locked or an action is denied, it means your role doesn't include that specific permission — ask your owner or administrator if you need it.

---

## "Access Restricted" — what it means

If you open Casino Settings and see a full-screen **Access Restricted** message instead of the company and properties, your role doesn't include permission to view this page. This is normal if you're not an administrator. Contact your administrator or the owner if you believe you need access.

---

## Tips

- **Changes aren't stored until you Save** — each section saves on its own.
- **A property always belongs to your company** — new properties are filed under your company automatically; you can't create one for another company.
- **Set the reporting currency carefully** — it's the currency a property's figures are recorded in, so getting it right up front avoids confusion later.
- **Deactivating is for record-keeping** — marking a company or property inactive is a status label; if you need a property to stop being used in day-to-day work, coordinate that with your administrator.
